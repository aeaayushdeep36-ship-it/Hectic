 Productivity
 Founders lose hours every week on repetitive work: market scans, lead lists, meeting summaries, competitor tracking, first drafts. The AI Agents for Founders Challenge invites higher-education students from around the world to build AI agents that take that work off their plate, and give them back time for what no agent should decide for them: judgment.
 YOUR MISSION 
 Design and ship an AI agent that saves measurable time on one concrete step of an entrepreneurial project (ideation, customer discovery, market research, lead generation, go-to-market, investor preparation...), while keeping a human firmly in charge of the decisions.
 WHAT MAKES THIS CHALLENGE DIFFERENT: EQUAL ACCESS TO AI 
 Every agent must run on AI models that any participant can use for free: open-weight models or free tiers. No paid credits, no premium subscriptions at runtime. The best agents are not the ones with the biggest budget, but the ones with the smartest design.
 WHY IT MATTERS 
 AI should not think for founders. It should free them to think better. That is why every agent must include at least one human checkpoint, and why we reward the teams that design for critical thinking.

 WHO CAN JOIN 
 Higher-education students aged 18 or over, worldwide, in teams of 2 to 4. No-code, low-code and code-based projects are equally welcome.
 ABOUT THE ORGANIZER 
 The challenge is hosted by HETIC, a Paris-based digital school, as part of its Master's program for future Chief Marketing Officers.
 Requirements
 WHAT TO BUILD 
 A working AI agent (or multi-step AI workflow) that: 
 1. automates or accelerates one clearly identified business task for early-stage founders; 
 2. includes at least one human checkpoint, where the user reviews, challenges or validates the agent's output before any decision or external action; 
 3. shows its reasoning or sources where relevant, so the user can check the output rather than trust it blindly; 
 4. complies with the Equal Access AI Policy below. 
 EQUAL ACCESS AI POLICY 
 - Runtime models: the agent must run exclusively on models that are free to access for any participant (open-weight models, or free tiers of commercial APIs). Paid credits, paid API plans and premium subscriptions are not allowed for the agent's runtime. 
 - AI-assisted building: using AI tools to help write code or design the workflow is allowed, but must be declared. 
 - No secrets: never publish API keys or credentials in your repository, video or workflow export. 
 - Verification: judges may ask any team for a live demo or additional evidence. Undeclared use of paid AI, or a false declaration, leads to disqualification. 
 WHAT TO SUBMIT ON DEVPOST 
 - A project page in English (detailed documentation may be in French, with an English summary). 
 - A demo video of 3 minutes maximum, uploaded to YouTube or Vimeo, showing a complete run of the agent, including the human checkpoint. 
 - A way for judges to test the agent at no cost: deployed link, test environment, or workflow export with setup instructions. 
 - A public code repository or workflow export, with installation instructions. 
 - An architecture diagram. 
 - An AI Disclosure Statement: every model, provider and tool used, its role in the agent, the plan used (free tier or open-weight), and an estimate of tokens or calls per run. 
 - A Time-Saved Report: the task before and after the agent, with a measured or reasonably estimated time saving. 
 - A Human Judgment section: what the agent does, what the human decides, and why. 
 - A Responsible AI section: data used, privacy, known limitations, bias and failure cases.

 Judging Criteria
 Business value & time saved 
 25% Does the agent solve a real founder problem, and is the time saving credible and measured?
 Human judgment by design 
 20% Does the agent strengthen the user's critical thinking instead of replacing it?
 Agent design & reliability 
 20% Is the workflow coherent, robust and reproducible? How does it handle errors?
 Frugality & accessibility 
 15% Does it make smart use of free models and resources? Could anyone reuse it?
 User experience 
 10% Can a founder use the agent without help from the team?
 Documentation & responsible AI 
 10% Is the project transparent, well documented and honest about its limits?
