# Responsible AI

Hectic is an independent student project submitted to the AI Agents for Founders
Challenge. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by HETIC.

This document answers the seven questions the brief asks of any product that uses
a language model. It is written to be checked, not to reassure.

---

## 1. What the model does

Four language steps, each with a versioned prompt in `agents/prompts/`:

| Step | Input | Output | Model class |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 `claim-formaliser` | one sentence of belief | population, predicate, kind, claim type, ambiguity flags | small |
| A2 `relevance-tagger` | redacted notes + claim predicate | `bears_on` and polarity per interview/claim | smallest |
| A3 `constraint-explainer` | an already-computed verdict | two factual sentences | small |
| A4 `statement-composer` | the locked ledger | method, coverage, findings, limitations prose | small |
| G1 `input-guard` | raw cells and notes | redacted text + injection flag | none (regex) |

Runtimes are free-tier or open-weight only: Gemini Flash-Lite, Groq
`llama-3.1-8b-instant`, Ollama `qwen2.5:7b`, and an in-repo deterministic mock.
The mock is the default and needs no key. No secret is stored in the repository.

## 2. What the model cannot do

It cannot produce a level, a count, a score, a threshold, a binding constraint
or a verdict. This is not a policy statement; it is structural:

- The A1 output schema has no numeric field (`lib/schemas.ts`).
- A2 emits only three string/boolean fields and is human-overridable.
- A3 rejects any generated digit that does not appear in the deterministic text.
- A4 receives a locked ledger and is checked against it.
- `engine/` has zero imports from any provider, from React, or from the database.
  `npm run engine:boundary` enforces this; swapping providers leaves
  `finding_hash` byte-identical (automated test, `tests/store.test.ts`).

## 3. Known failure modes

| Failure | Direction of error | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| A1 widens the population (e.g. "practice managers" → "healthcare professionals") | Makes the sample look *more* adequate | Mandatory human confirmation; every field editable; ambiguity flags shown |
| A2 finds relevance everywhere | Inflates counts | `bears_on: false` prompt discipline; visible tags; human override stored with `overridden_by_user` |
| A3/A4 invent a number | Misleading artefact | Numeric guard falls back to deterministic template |
| Person names in free text | PII reaches a provider | Names are **not** detected; UI asks for aliases; documented limitation |
| Injection in notes or claim text | Tag pollution only | Pattern screen, JSON encoding, structural separation, and no numeric field to corrupt |

## 4. Who is affected

The founder's interviewees — people who never heard of this product and did not
consent to AI processing. Mitigations: local-only operation, no account, no
telemetry, aliases instead of names, redaction before any provider call, and
notes excluded from the export entirely.

## 5. What the tool must not be used for

- As legal, financial or investment advice.
- As a substitute for a research method.
- To certify evidence the founder knows to be fabricated.
- As a "market size" or "TAM" generator. Hectic audits discovery evidence only.

## 6. AI-assisted development

The engine, thresholds, database schema, API routes, UI components and tests
were written with AI assistance and reviewed against the specifications in
`docs/`. The research in `research/` was conducted with web fetches and Python
batteries, also AI-assisted. All citations were fetched from primary sources or
publisher pages; the Pattyn paper was read in full. No part of the product was
generated by a third-party service at runtime without disclosure above.

## 7. Limits of the certification

Printed on the artefact itself, not buried here:

> A level means "the strongest evidence type present, supported by this many
> independent organisations" — not "the conclusion is true". Pattyn et al. is an
> exploratory study of 16 startups, not a law. Saturation figures transfer from
> other research settings. The Wilson interval assumes random sampling that a
> founder's network never gives; it is a best case, and true uncertainty is wider.

The full falsification audit of the original scoring ladder is in
`research/03-engine-findings.md`.
