# ambiguous — the gate fixture — hand computation

- 12 discovery interviews from 12 distinct organisations, each L2 stated.
- One signed letter of intent from a budget holder at a 13th organisation,
  L4 commitment. It came through a founder relationship — a diagnostic, not a
  gate.
- All 13 organisations are in the claim's population (Dental practices).

Per organisation: `types = { 2: 12, 4: 1 }`.

Therefore: `level = 4`, `support = 1`, `breadth = 13`, saturation `null`
(level is above the stated-evidence range). Claimed level is L5 →
`DOWNGRADE` (and L5 is investor commitment, which never validates a customer
claim anyway).

The founder declares 1 of 1 approached organisation committed. Wilson 95%:
`[0.21, 1.00]`, width `0.79` — a best case, not a market rate.

Support is 1, below the minimum of 3, so binding is `support`: repeat the
commitment on more independent organisations.

**Why this is the gate:** there is no honest scalar for this case. Twelve
interviews plus one LOI is not "about L2.5". It is L4 resting on one
organisation, and the twelve L2 interviews are a different *kind* of evidence,
not weight behind the LOI. Any refactor that blends them fails the fixture.
